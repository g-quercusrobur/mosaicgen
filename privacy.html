Privacy and legal

Version of 16 October 2026 · Siderarium, the macrocosmic clock

Who is responsible

This site and the Siderarium app are published by Hippocrene Design Works LLC, which is the data controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and comparable laws.

What the app does with your data

  • Nothing leaves your device. The app computes the sky in your browser. Your settings, your birth data (date, time and place) and your saved charts are kept in the browser's local storage on your device. They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else.
  • No cookies. The site and the app set no cookies. Local storage is used only for the settings you choose, which is strictly necessary for the app to remember them; for this reason no consent banner is shown.
  • No analytics, no trackers, no third-party requests. The fonts are served with the app rather than from a font service.
  • Location. The "here" button asks your browser for your position so that the horizon of the dial can be your own. The browser asks your permission first; the coordinates are used on the device only and can be cleared by choosing a city instead.

Hosting

The site and the app are served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses to operate the service and meet its legal obligations, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive these logs.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side; everything the app knows about you is on your device and under your control. If you have a question under the GDPR you can write to Hippocrene Design Works LLC. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.
