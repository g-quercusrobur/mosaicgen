# cosmo weather — system book

A cosmic clock. It renders the sky of the moment as an instrument: the frame is Bauhaus, the content is the trip.
Every mark on the dial is computed from the sky by a stated rule. Nothing is ornament.

---

## 1 · Principles

1. **Austere frame, maximal content.** Grid, type and interface are strict and colourless. Everything inside the circle is computed light.
2. **One variable, one channel.** No astrological factor drives more than one visual channel, and no channel takes more than one factor.
3. **One authority per channel.** Golden Dawn decides colour. The Bauhaus (Itten, Albers) decides structure and colour harmony. Lilly decides dignity.
4. **Traceable.** Any mark can be explained from the readout. The readout is part of the product.
5. **Earned excess.** Stars, mandalas and portals appear only when the sky produces them.

## 4 · The instrument

- **Container.** A dodecagon, Saturn's black, edged in lead. Twelve sides hold every Ptolemaic aspect as a vertex subset (2, 3, 4, 6, 12). Quintiles and septiles do not fit it and look foreign against it, which is correct.
- **Houses.** Vertices are cusps and faces are houses: ascendant on the left vertex, MC on the top vertex. The frame is fixed to the observer's horizon. It does not turn.
- **Rete.** A monochrome chapter ring: degree ticks and sign glyphs in off-white, rotated so the ascendant degree sits on the eastern horizon. Planet notches in their tinctures mark true longitude. Sign colour never appears on the frame; it lives in the field and the auras.
- **Sky.** Planets are placed in their real local sky. The angle is the Placidian mundane position, the same unequal division as the planetary hours: rising on the left, culminating at the top, setting on the right. The radius is the altitude. The noon sun stands high in summer and low in winter, as on the Prague Orloj. Below the horizon the sun is switched off.
- **Portal.** The inner disc, half the sky's radius, centred on the pole. Mandalas live here and only here.
- **Pole.** The axis. Coloured by the day ruler and the hour ruler.

## 5 · Mandalas

An aspect is a fraction of the circle: angle = k·360/n → the star polygon {n/k}.

| aspect | {n/k} | figure |
|---|---|---|
| conjunction | 1 | rose of P petals, P = sum of the two planets' numbers (sun 6 + venus 7 = 13) |
| opposition | {2/1} | vesica piscis on the axis |
| trine | {3/1} | triangle |
| square | {4/1} | square |
| sextile | {6/1} | hexagram, seed of life when soft |
| quintile / biquintile | {5/1} {5/2} | pentagon / pentagram |
| septiles | {7/1} {7/2} {7/3} | heptagons |
| semisquare / sesquisquare | {8/1} {8/3} | octagons |
| quincunx | {12/5} | twelve-point star |

**Hardness.** Soft aspects bow their edges into petals and add a circle chain through the pole. Hard aspects keep straight edges with points at the vertices. Benefics (venus, jupiter) soften; malefics (mars, saturn, pluto, uranus) sharpen.

**Rose engine.** The primary figure is wrapped in a guilloché band: 5 to 9 passes of r(θ) = R·(1 − a + a·f(Pθ)), where f morphs from a cosine (round petals) to a cusp (spikes) with hardness. A second band at 2P, counter-phased, makes the moiré.

**Two colours.** The lines carry the planets, the fill carries the relationship. Planet A's rose-engine passes twist clockwise in A's colour and planet B's twist counter-clockwise in B's, so the engraving crosses as two forces. Every star edge passes from one planet's colour to the other's: smoothly when soft, with a hard split at the midpoint when hard. Circle chains alternate. White appears only when a planet demands it (neptune's brilliance, uranus's pale iridescence).

**Colour law for the fill (Itten chords).**
- soft, fusion, creative: **light**. Additive, interpolated in OKLab.
- hard: **pigment**. Multiplicative in linear RGB, chroma pulled down. Mud.
- anything mixed with saturn goes black. Black figures are drawn as grooves carved into the light, edged by the other planet's colour.
- pluto lends no pigment; it bends the space instead.

**Orientation.** Vertex zero points at the first planet's true longitude on the rete. Rotation comes from the sky, never from a design choice.

**Constellations.** Figures share the portal at the same radius. Only where vertices of two figures coincide: a white star with a diffraction cross. Plain edge crossings do not count (tested: they read as beads). Major stars are joined by a hairline: the figure of the day. Two centred, aligned figures share gcd(n₁, n₂) vertices.

## 5b · Size, cut, guardians

- **Size.** Jupiter owns scale and Saturn owns restriction. e = 0.3·(jupiter score − saturn score)/10, plus 0.6 if jupiter is in the figure, minus 0.6 if saturn is, plus 0.15 for venus or sun, minus 0.3 for pluto. Scale = 1 + 0.45e, clamped to 0.5–1.5. When jupiter slows, the expansion is halved: it turns inward. All figures share the portal radius, so constellations still form.
- **Cut.** The strongest hard aspect (square, opposition, quincunx, semi- or sesquisquare) from a third planet to exactly one planet of the figure removes a wedge. The wedge points at the afflicting planet, its width is 24–100° by power, and its edges are hairlines in the afflictor's colour. The moon never cuts.
- **Guardians.** The day ruler and the hour ruler as their planetary stars: saturn triangle, jupiter square, mars pentagram, sun hexagram, venus heptagram {7/3}, mercury octagram {8/3}, moon enneagram {9/4}. They stand point up, inside a soft light; the pole is no longer a point. One ruler (the first hour of every day) gives one pure figure; two rulers overlap and share gcd(n₁, n₂) points.

## 6b · State rings

Every planet wears a ring whose form is its essential condition: whole for domicile, doubled for exaltation, thin for minor dignity (triplicity, term, face), faint for peregrine, broken into segments for detriment (exile), broken and drawn in for fall. The colour is the sign, purity follows the dignity score, retrograde blurs it, combust bleaches it. Outer planets wear the same forms in their optics: uranus iridescent, neptune white, pluto a hairline.

## 6c · Ripeness: the moon's electional condition

Shown at the centre, where the rulers of the hour already sit, and stated in the caption.

- **next aspect** = the first Ptolemaic aspect the moon will perfect with a classical planet before leaving its sign. None = **void of course**.
- **afflicted**: applying to mars or saturn by conjunction, square or opposition. **under the beams** within 12° of the sun, **combust** within 8.5°. **via combusta** 15° libra – 15° scorpio.
- **helped**: applying to venus or jupiter, or applying by trine or sextile.

| verdict | centre |
|---|---|
| ripe | the light burns warm and bright |
| open | normal light |
| unripe | the light dims and turns cold, the guardians fade |
| void | the light goes out: a dark pupil, the guardians as ghost hairlines. the door is closed |

Caption, right column: `moon applying square saturn · 37%` or `moon void of course · 28%`. The planetary day begins at sunrise, so before dawn the caption can read "saturday · day of venus". That is correct.

## 7 · Hierarchy

power = (0.2 + 0.8 · rarity) × aspect weight × (0.3 · exactness^1.5 + 0.7 · window) × modifiers

- **rarity** = log of the pair's synodic period, from the moon (a month) to neptune–pluto (492 years), normalized.
- **window** is measured in time, not degrees: exp(−½(days to exact / 1.5)²). Slow pairs linger in orb for years. They open the portal only in the days around an exact pass.
- **aspect weights** conjunction 1.0 · opposition .9 · square .8 · trine .7 · sextile .5 · quincunx .35 · minor .26–.32
- **modifiers** applying ×1.2, separating ×0.8, station ×1.5, sun involved ×1.2, angular ×1.3
- **events override:** eclipse > lunation > cazimi > outer-planet exact > station
- **the moon takes full part.** Its windows are hours (0.25 days), not days, so it rises only near exactness. It may cut the figure, but only by aspects it is applying to (hard aspects, or conjunction with mars or saturn). Flicker is accepted: it is true.
- one primary figure, at most two secondary figures. Below 0.25 the primary shrinks: a quiet sky.

## 8 · Field

A colour field, not a texture. Three lights over midnight: the ascendant from the eastern horizon, the sun and the moon at their real positions, each in its sign colour and weighted by sect. Below the horizon the field is slightly darker: under the earth. No noise, no smoke, no photographs. Every structure inside the circle is computed geometry.

Rejected and why: a noise nebula (encodes nothing) and a planetary interference field (nine summed wave sources look like random blobs, so it reads as noise anyway). Both remain reachable for comparison with `?field=smoke`.

**Harris weave.** One figure spanning the two luminaries: the circles through both sun and moon, drawn only between them, as a spindle of arcs passing from gold to silver. Projective geometry in the manner of Frieda Harris's Thoth backgrounds, computed from where the two actually stand. At new moon it closes into rings; at full moon it spans the dial. The sun's prismatic rays were removed: from the viewer's side they read as "some planets emit a grid".

## 13 · Round 5 changes

- **One projection.** Every body sits at its ecliptic longitude on the same angle as the rete, with the ascendant on the eastern horizon; its distance from the centre is its altitude. (Earlier the bodies used a time-based house projection while the ring used longitude, so a planet could sit beside the wrong sign glyph. The data were right; the drawing contradicted itself.)
- **Equal houses** from the ascendant. With the ascendant fixed on the left, the regular dodecagon *is* the equal-house frame. The MC is drawn where it actually falls, as a pointer across the container.
- **Planets as polygons.** Core = the planet's own polygon (saturn 3 … mercury 8), point up. Ring = the same polygon, larger, whose form shows the condition (§6b) and whose rotation shows the alignment: aligned for domicile and exaltation, a quarter-step off for minor dignity, half off for peregrine, a full half-step (vertex against edge) for detriment and fall. Size grows with power (dignity, angularity, station, part in the figure), up to about 1.6×.
- **Contrast.** Darker ground, stronger lights, solid line cores, a deeper vignette. Portal base radius 0.56 R.
- **The luminaries' field.** The weave is the flow of a source (sun) into a sink (moon): in the plane, a source–sink pair's streamlines are exactly the circles through both points, and the equipotentials are the Apollonian circles. Particles run along the streamlines from gold to silver; their pace follows the moon's light. Rings around the moon draw the cup. At new moon source and sink coincide and nothing flows.
- **Chord.** Each planet of the figure sounds the note of the sign it stands in (Paul Foster Case's colour–note scale: the King-scale colour wheel read as the chromatic scale, aries = C). Three or more notes forming a known chord are named (C7, G-, Gmaj7…); otherwise the figure's two planets give a dyad, first > second. Aspects become intervals: opposition = tritone, square = minor third, trine = major third, sextile = whole tone.
- **Sigil layer.** Veils the sky and draws the ruling figure, its cut and the guardian stars in solid ink, for copying.
- **The next 24 hours.** A strip under the dial plots three windows, from the three mother letters: ideation (aleph, air), incarnation (mem, water: descent into form), destruction (shin, fire). Each window combines the figure's planets and signs, the figure's nature, and the beauty of the moment (whole, harmonious guardians, ripe moon; destruction takes the opposite). A slider previews any minute in the next 24 hours, and the next peak of each window over the coming week is listed: tap to go there.
- **Chart mode.** No field. Signs as full sectors in their colours, equal houses, the meridian drawn, aspects in their own colours (opposition red, square orange, trine blue, sextile teal, quincunx green dashed, minor aspects dotted), and leader lines from each planet's exact degree to its glyph.
- **Next chord shift.** The caption's right column ends with `next chord F#ø · fri 16 oct 16:23`. The engine walks forward in 10-minute steps until the chord's name changes, then halves the interval to the minute. It looks a week ahead and takes well under a second.
- **Chord, revised.** The chord is the sky's harmony: the notes of the two strongest figures, moon included, weighed by the sky's own weight (no angularity: the turning horizon changed the ranking every few hours and the chord with it). Measured over two weeks: about 2.7 changes a day, median hold almost 5 hours, a named chord about a third of the time.
- **Motion.** Running, the clock advances with the real frame interval and redraws every frame the screen gives. Blurs and the eclipse search are skipped and the heavy readings (readout, week scan, next chord) wait until it rests. Speeds: 15 min, 1 h, 4 h, 24 h of sky per second. The run button becomes pause. Choosing a window stops the clock on that moment.
- **Place.** On the first visit the dial takes the city of the device's own time zone at once (no permission needed), then asks for the device's position and uses it if granted. "here" asks again. The city menu holds one city for each time zone, west to east, with its current offset.
- **Listen.** A button sounds the chord as a slow organ drone: sine partials 1–4 for each note (pure tone plus overtones, the organ pipe), each doubled with a ±0.12 Hz detune so it breathes. The root sounds again an octave below. A = 440: the 432 Hz "cosmic tuning" is avoided on purpose. Swell 3 s, low-pass at 2.2 kHz, a generated 3-second reverb. While on, the drone glides to the next chord when the sky shifts, so the harmony moves audibly while the clock runs.
- **Round 7.** Font: Geist and Geist Mono (one family; the wordmark is Geist with wide tracking). Caption and frame type scale up on small screens; exports keep the master size. The date field is typed in the place's own time (yyyy-mm-dd hh:mm). Turning the dial travels in time: clockwise is forward, one turn is one day, a wheel notch is ten minutes. The readout uses full words. An about section gives the concept, history, methods and references. Outer planets carry no essential dignity. Every planet within 17° of the sun is under the beams. Sigil lines thinner. An engraved field (light as line weight, no glows) is available as a prototype behind `?engrave=1`.
- **Zoom.** Two fingers pinch and pan; ctrl/⌘ + wheel (or a trackpad pinch) zooms at the pointer; + and − keys; double click or double tap returns to the whole dial. Up to 8×. The plate is redrawn at the zoomed scale, so lines stay sharp. One finger and the plain wheel still move time. Engraving lines 20% coarser, so the engraving shows at screen size.

## 15 · Round 9 changes

- **Houses: Regiomontanus**, Lilly's system. The equator is divided into twelve from the meridian and each division is carried to the ecliptic by a great circle through the north and south points of the horizon; such a circle is the horizon of a latitude whose tangent is tan(φ)·sin(H), so each cusp is an ascendant computed for that pole. The MC is the cusp of the 10th, where astrologers expect it. The regular dodecagon stays as the container; the cusps are drawn across its band where they fall, the angles stronger, the number mid-house. Equal houses and Placidus remain behind `?houses=equal` and `?houses=placidus`. The MC itself still sits where the sky puts it on a uniform zodiac: putting it at the top would bend the zodiac and the figures with it.
- **Conjunctions are one form.** Bodies joined by a conjunction aspect are grouped; the group is drawn closed on its common centre until the rings overlap, the rings fuse into a single outline (stroked on a scratch plate, interiors erased), the solid bodies fill as one material mixed from their tinctures by power, and the glyphs stand side by side outside the form in zodiacal order. Optics (Uranus, Neptune, Pluto) keep their own effects inside the fused ring. At a cazimi the Sun's hexagon simply swallows the planet.
- **Pluto occults.** The magnifying lens is gone. Inside Pluto's circle (its own state ring) the field is put out and only the structure remains, as under an eclipse; at the limb a corona of streamers in the colour sampled from what it covers, lit by its power. No magnification, no pigment of its own. In the figure, the figure's heart goes into eclipse; at a station, a corona rings the whole portal.
- **Void Moon:** the pupil at the pole is near black and the whole portal dims around it; the guardians' star fades to a ghost.
- **Wallpaper:** no wheel. The field, and the engraving, run to the edges of a portrait canvas the size of the phone; the dial is sized to the width and set below the lock-screen clock. No caption.
- **Prototype, not built:** `?bodies=luminous` draws the planets as lights instead of bodies: flat tincture, glowing by power, no sheen or shadow. For comparison against the metallic default.

## 17 · Name and glyph

- **Siderarium**, "the room of the stars", on the pattern of planetarium. The descriptor: *macrocosmic clock visually tracking the astrological energy of the moment*. The three modes are the Hermetic pair: clock is the macrocosm, natal the microcosm, transits one laid on the other. The folder and the Instagram feed keep "cosmo weather" for now.

The icon is a vesica piscis (the door, the lens, Euclid I.1) holding the heptagram {7/3}: the seven planets in Chaldean order joined every third point give the days of the week, so the star is the clock of the week drawn from the planetary order. Forest green on the void. The vesica is drawn as a double tressure, a heavy line with a hairline within, in the broader proportion of a seal's mandorla; the centre is a bezant in an annulet, the light of the moment. Heraldic by a tad, no more.
