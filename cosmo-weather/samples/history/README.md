Reading: five of ten moments fall in the top fifth, three of them on exact slow-planet contacts known in mundane astrology (jupiter–uranus 1969, saturn–neptune 1989, neptune–pluto 1957). The nuclear moments of 1942–45 score at or below chance. With ten hand-picked events the excess is suggestive (about p ≈ 0.06), not proof; selection by fame is itself a bias.
